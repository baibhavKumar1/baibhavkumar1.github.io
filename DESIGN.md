# DESIGN.md — Baibhav Kumar Portfolio (Warm Tactile Editorial Studio)

## Aesthetic Identity: "Warm Tactile Editorial Studio"

An editorial, human-centered portfolio for a Senior / Staff Fullstack AI Engineer & GenAI Systems Architect. Stripped of cold robotic cyber aesthetics, neon glowing dots, and generic terminal defaults. Replaced with warm tactile surfaces, rich editorial typography, generous whitespace, and an authentic engineering journal feel.

---

## 1. Subject & Strategic Goal

- **Subject**: Baibhav Kumar (Senior / Staff Fullstack AI Engineer & GenAI Systems Architect)
- **Target Audience**: CTOs, Engineering VPs, and Technical Founders evaluating candidates for 25–60 LPA ($150K+) roles.
- **Page's Single Job**: Inspire immediate confidence in technical depth (distributed WhatsApp CoEx, remote MCP hosts, hybrid audio caching, mobile Genkit integration) and human-centered design craft through an inviting, editorial experience.

---

## 4. The Signature Element (Ditching Templates: Shaders & Dithering)

The portfolio rejects flat, rectangular templates. To visually prove frontend, backend, fullstack, and AI capabilities, we incorporate custom-engineered graphics:

### **WebGL GLSL Custom Fragment Shaders**
- An interactive background element that compiles a low-overhead custom fragment shader.
- Slowly-drifting, ambient noise loops that represent the stateful AI flows.
- **The Bayer Halftone Flex**: The shader calculates a 4x4 Bayer Dither matrix in GPU registers to discretize gradients into physical-looking retro dots in real-time, responding to user scroll.

### **SVG Halftone & Bayer-Dither Filters**
- Custom SVG filters configured inside CSS rules.
- Applied to illustrations, schematic node cards, and heading text to create a tactile digital print look that feels like a premium technical blueprint.

### **"The Interactive Architecture Field Note"**
An editorial system design entry displayed as an open, annotated engineering document in the hero section:
- **Hero Statement**: *"Architecting GenAI Systems with Human Craft & Systems Rigor."*
- **Interactive Field Note**: Visitors explore annotated blueprints for the Coexistence WhatsApp Automation Engine, Cloudery MCP Edge Routing, and MusicBuddy's MD5 Deduplication Cache.
- **Interactive Trace Streams**: Custom simulations of live state changes, demonstrating how the systems handle load, interruptions, and caching events.

## 6. Design Principles & Anti-Template Rules

1. **Warmth Over Coldness**: Natural cream linen background (`#FAF8F5`), soft rounded corners (`12px`–`16px`), and warm organic borders replacing harsh dark mode defaults.
2. **Editorial Typography**: Elegant serif headings (`Fraunces` / `Newsreader`) paired with clean humanist sans body text (`Plus Jakarta Sans`) for a readable, premium publication feel.
3. **Authentic Terminology**: Use real engineering terms (*Architecture Field Note*, *System Blueprint*, *Trade-Off Ledger*, *Human-in-the-Loop Interrupt*) instead of generic software tropes like "workbench", "dashboard", or "console".
4. **Accessible & Ergonomic**: High contrast ratios (7:1+), visible warm amber focus outlines, and full responsive scaling across all viewports.
